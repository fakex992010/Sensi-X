      📖 How SensiX Works

      1. Hardware detection

      Your screen's refresh rate (Hz) drives responsiveness. SensiX boosts sensitivity on 60 Hz screens so your aim keeps up.

      2. Playstyle physics

      Freestyle and Aggressive need high General + Red Dot for drag shots. Sniper needs low AWM for micro-adjustments. Headshot pushes Red Dot very high for one-taps.

      3. DPI + Button sizes

      Lower Hz = higher DPI. Button sizes are tuned per playstyle — bigger fire button for rushers, smaller for snipers.

      4. HUD recommendation

      Your finger count changes the layout. 3-finger claw is for rushers, 4-finger is the competitive standard, 5-finger is for freestyle pros.
